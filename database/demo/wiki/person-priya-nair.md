# Priya Nair

Priya Nair is [[project:ledgerline|Ledgerline]]'s contract designer. She started on the
invoice PDF template, then redesigned onboarding, and is now designing the invoice
editor rework. She invoices Rowan through Ledgerline itself, so she uses the
product from both sides.

## Key facts
- Contract, about 10 hours a week at the start, down to 6 hours since the template
  and onboarding shipped.
- Rebuilt the invoice template with tables after the switch to a pure-PHP PDF
  renderer, which has no CSS grid.
- Caught that the template's original font licence did not cover embedding in PDFs.

## Work shipped
- **Invoice template:** amount due and due date at top right, line items as a plain
  table, payment details boxed at the bottom. Customers liked it; there is no data
  on whether their clients pay faster.
- **Onboarding:** five steps down to two (business details, first client) with the
  first invoice prefilled. Trial-to-paid went from 5 of 30 in the month before to
  9 of 31 since.

## Open items
- Invoice editor mockups delivered 10 days ago: editable line-item rows with inline
  totals, the client picker at the top, a sticky Send bar.
