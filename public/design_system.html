§ 3 — Typography
Three families. EB Garamond leads.
Display in serif, body in Inter, measurements in mono.

Headlines may include one italic vermilion word. Never two.
3.2 — Body & ledeEB Garamond + Inter

MM Drop caps mark the lede paragraph in long-form articles. Garamond, weight 600, vermilion, floated 5.4em tall. Used once per article.

Body prose in long-form runs at 1.125rem in Garamond regular. Lines breathe at 1.65 line-height. Italics carry the marginal voice — memoria artificiosa — without resorting to bold.

UI body, including this paragraph, is set in Inter at 1rem on 1.55. It carries supplementary information, navigation, and shorter copy that does not warrant Garamond's authority.
